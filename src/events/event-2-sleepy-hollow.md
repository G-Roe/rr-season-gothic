> A Story Game based on the Legend of Sleepy Hollow.

For centuries stories have been passed down through oral tradition. Some as pedantic tales of warning or frightful yarns to entertain. For every story there is a kernel of truth buried under layers of interpretation. Only the Raspy Ravens judge their voracity.

Those cursed with the wyrding way or good folks who have gone through great tragedy can hear them. In the babble of a brook, between drops of rain, or in the moan of creaking trees on the moors. Inching closer to madness.

A conspiracy has gathered to bear witness once again for the Legend of Sleepy Hollow as retold by a vexation of goblyns. A story, within a tale, within a bloody sentence.
