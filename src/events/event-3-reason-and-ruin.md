IT IS 1920. YOU HAVE RETURNED TO INGARSBY HALL, the last remnant of a deserted Leicestershire village. 

Beneath the Hall, something ancient persists: among flooded cellars, sealed rooms, and a forgotten tragedy. 
Some cling to Reason (knowledge, restraint, order) the cold assurances of waking life, others surrender to 
Ruin (passion, obsession, impulse) and the strange meaning in dreams. 

Together you will uncover buried histories, confront the darkness in Ingarsby Hall and risk descent into memory, obsession, and corruption.
