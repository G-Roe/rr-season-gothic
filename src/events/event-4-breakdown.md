When a violent storm forces a young couple’s car off a country road they seek help at remote farmhouse. But the storm isn’t the real danger and once inside Blackwood Farm safety is an illusion that won’t last the night.

This game explores what happens when the fictions people hold about themselves come under pressure. This is not a game about who gets the loot. This is a game about how people break and what happens when they do.

> Content: Breakdown is a game of modern Gothic sensibilities. It strives to explore secrets, crimes and traumas of the past in an oppressive and isolated setting. It is the darker palette of humanity. The tragic currents of compulsion and guilt, the blurred certainty of sanity, truth, madness and lies. The grim shadows that lie between obsessive love and abusive control. There are elements that some people may find upsetting.
