Somewhere in 19th Century Europe is a fog-shrouded country dotted with gloomy manor houses, looming castles and dark forests. Dark creatures inhabit this place, and villagers cower in fear of the monsters that stalk the night. But you know that there’s a difference between a monster and a villain.

What matters isn’t what you are – it’s what you do. And you're not just any monster hunters. You're gothic sword lesbians. Will you travel through the darkness of Evenshade Forest, uncover the secrets of Malapeste Manor, or come face to face with the horrors of Castle Grauerstein? We'll choose a campaign concept and adventure seed, and build our characters and scenario from there.

> Content warnings: romance, kissing, supernatural threats and violence. As this will be heavily improvised, anything else will be based on lines and veils discussion.
