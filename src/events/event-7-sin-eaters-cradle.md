A family is lost in the California Redwoods. A search party has until dark to find them.
Something has been sleeping beneath these trees for longer than any name given to this land. The mill brought roads. The roads brought people. The people brought grief, and their grief was useful.

A mystery of inherited dread, industrial rot, and corrupted faith set in 1928 along the banks of the Klamath River. Four investigators. One ticking clock.

> Content Warnings: Violence, gore, cannibalism, offscreen harm to children and animals, grief, and religious extremism.
